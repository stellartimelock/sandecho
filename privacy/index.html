Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers sandecho.com and the tools behind it. It is run by Stellar TimeLock LLC dba Sand Echo Media ("we", "us"). We keep it short because we collect very little.

Browsing this site

This site sets no cookies and has no ads, trackers, or social media pixels. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and load times without cookies and without tracking you across other sites. Like any website, Cloudflare (our network and security provider) and GitHub Pages (our host) process standard request data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site.

Quote form and contacting us

If you use the quote form, we receive what you type: your name, email, business name, website, the service you want, and your message. The form is sent through our own small Cloudflare Worker, which emails it to sandechomedia@gmail.com. The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-focused spam check that may process basic browser signals to tell people from bots. If you email us, we receive your message and email address.

We use this information only to answer you, quote and do the work you ask for, and send related invoices. We do not sell, rent, or share it for marketing.

Clients and invoicing

For clients, we keep the business details needed to run and bill your website service (contact name, billing email, plan, and payment records). This is stored in a private dashboard that only the owner of Sand Echo Media can sign in to.

Google account data (Gmail)

Our private dashboard at app.sandecho.com can connect to the Gmail account of Sand Echo Media's owner. It is used only by the owner, for his own mailbox. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's Google account can be connected.

  • What it accesses: the Gmail "compose" permission (gmail.compose), used only to create invoice drafts in the owner's own mailbox. It does not read, search, or delete email, and the dashboard never sends email on its own. The owner reviews and sends each draft himself.
  • What is stored: encrypted Google sign-in tokens kept on our server so the dashboard can create drafts. No email contents are stored.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared, sold, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Revoking: access can be disconnected at any time in the dashboard or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting in the dashboard revokes and deletes the stored tokens.

Sand Echo Media's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep quote requests and client records only as long as needed to work with you and meet tax and accounting needs. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
